03 Personnel file

About Emre Melik Nameli

I am Emre Melik Nameli — also known as ROXSMB — an independent 3D character artist, animator and tool developer. My work covers stylized and semi-realistic characters, game-ready modeling, PBR texturing, rigging and Unreal Engine integration.

I care about the parts that make art usable: topology that deforms, UVs that hold up, material budgets that survive a real-time frame. Alongside client work I build artist-first software.
